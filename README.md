# KDL Target Setting Analysis

An Excel-based case study exploring how lifecycle, seasonal crop mix, and margin assumptions shape sales target design.

## Business question

How should target acres and category mix vary by KDL lifecycle while supporting a blended margin objective?

## What the workbook contains

- **Overview:** headline scenario metrics and recommended review areas.
- **Target Model:** lifecycle acreage targets, modeled segment economics, category sales mix, and crop allocations.
- **Margin Review:** grouped reference margin statistics by product category.
- **Method & Checks:** metric definitions, source notes, and data-quality review.
- **Analysis Trail:** the question → source → calculation → evidence → interpretation chain, including validation and limits.

## Selected findings

- In the under-six-month baseline scenario, fertilizer represents about 81% of monthly sales and 39% of modeled monthly margin.
- The non-fertilizer segment represents about 19% of sales and 61% of modeled monthly margin.
- Reference product margins vary by category; five unclassified items have unusually high margins and need review before category planning.

## Approach

1. Translated the target-setting question into measurable lifecycle acreage targets.
2. Compared the modeled sales mix with margin contribution to identify the blend trade-off.
3. Reviewed product-category margin references and seasonal crop mix.
4. Checked incomplete and unclassified source records, and flagged an ambiguous lifecycle label.
5. Framed follow-up actions while distinguishing planning assumptions from realized outcomes.

## Interpretation and limitations

Values in the workbook are planning-model outputs, not actual sales outcomes. Category-level reference averages are unweighted and are not realized, sales-weighted margins. Crop-season acreage lines can overlap. The workbook excludes personal contact details, location-level operational records, and row-level product prices, but contains aggregated internal planning assumptions. Confirm that those assumptions are approved for public release before publishing this repository.
